Half of labeling quality is communication: is this a Product or a Price Tag, does the guideline cover this edge case, why was this task rejected? PixlHub keeps those conversations pinned to the exact pixels they are about, instead of losing them in chat apps three tools away.

<Info>
Comments are available to every project role, on every task. Reviewers use the same mechanism to explain rejections, so labelers see feedback in place rather than in a status field.
</Info>

## Pin a comment to a location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the Comment tool">
    Click the comment icon at the bottom of the floating palette (or press C).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the spot">
    Click where the question lives: the ambiguous object, the blurry region, the price tag in dispute. A pin appears and the composer opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your note. Mention a teammate with @ to notify them. Send with the shortcut shown in the composer (Ctrl or Cmd + Enter) or the send button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow the thread

Every pinned comment also lives in the **Comments** panel on the right rail, with filters for All, Open and Resolved. Replies keep the discussion threaded under the original pin, and the avatar marker stays on the canvas so anyone opening the task sees where the conversation points.

<Frame caption="The Comments panel: open and resolved filters, threaded replies, and the same pin mirrored on the canvas.">
  <img src="/images/docs/editor/comment-thread.jpg" alt="PixlHub Comments panel showing an open threaded comment about price labels" />
</Frame>

Unread activity is easy to spot: the Comments tab shows a notification dot when a task has discussion you have not seen.

## Comments that actually help

- **Point at the pixels.** "The third shelf label from the left" is a sentence; a pin is an answer. Place the pin on the object, not near it.
- **Mention the person who can decide.** An @ to the project manager turns a stalled question into a notification in their queue.
- **Resolve when settled.** Open threads are a to-do list; resolving keeps the Open filter meaningful for reviewers sweeping a project.
- **Guideline gaps become comments, comments become guidelines.** When the same question appears on the third task, the answer belongs in the project Guidelines, one click away in the right rail.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who sees my comments?">
    Everyone with access to the task: labelers assigned to it, reviewers and project managers. Comments are collaboration, not private notes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do comments affect the exported data?">
    No. Comments are workflow metadata, not annotations, so exports stay clean. They remain attached to the task for audit and context.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I comment without a location?">
    Task-level discussion lives in the same Comments panel; the pin is optional context. Use pinned comments whenever the question is about a specific region, which in practice is most of the time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
