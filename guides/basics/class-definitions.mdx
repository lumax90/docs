The top-left panel of the editor is the schema in action: every class from your [Label Schema](/guides/essentials/label-schema) with its color, its hotkey, and a live count of how many annotations of that class the current task carries.

</Frame>

## Arming a class

Whatever you draw takes the **armed** class, shown highlighted in the panel. Two ways to arm:

- **Click** the class row.
- **Press its hotkey**, the number shown as "Hot: 1", "Hot: 2" and so on (shortcuts 1 through 5 select labels, per the [shortcut reference](/guides/basics/interactive-toolbar)).

Arming also reclassifies: select an existing annotation with the Select tool and press a different number, and the annotation switches class.

## Reading the panel

- The **count** on each row is per-task and live; when guidelines say "label every price tag", this is the scoreboard.
- The **ACTIVE chip** in the header confirms how many classes the schema exposes here.
- The **row menu** (the dots on hover) carries per-class actions without a trip back to the schema tab.

## In dense scenes

The counts turn coverage into arithmetic: eight vehicles visible, six on the counter, two to go. Combined with [Object Instances'](/guides/basics/object-instances) per-object visibility toggles, working one class at a time through a crowded frame stays tractable: arm, sweep the frame, check the count, next class.

<Info>
Panel order equals hotkey order, and both come from the schema; put the highest-volume classes first when [designing it](/guides/essentials/label-schema).
</Info>
