Rendering preferences are personal and task-dependent: dense scenes want thin borders and low opacity, precision work wants a crosshair, demos want big labels. **Canvas Settings** (the gear at the bottom right of the editor, also on the right rail) is where you tune the canvas without touching the data.

## Display toggles

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show Labels** | Class chips on annotations; turn off to see pure geometry |
| **Center Labels** | Moves chips from the corner to the shape's center |
| **Crosshair** | Full-canvas guide lines that follow the cursor, for precise alignment |
| **Lock Tool Selection** | Keeps the active tool from resetting between tasks |

## Annotation rendering

Five sliders control how shapes draw, all cosmetic and instant:

- **Opacity** (default 85%) fills shape interiors; drop it when annotations hide the pixels you still need to judge.
- **Border** (1.5x) is stroke weight; heavier reads better on busy imagery, thinner on dense scenes.
- **Points** (6px) and **Anchors** (5px) size the point markers and the vertex handles on selected shapes.
- **Freeform** (10) controls freeform stroke rendering.

<Info>
Settings are cosmetic only and apply per project, so your retail project and your street project can keep different tuning. Exports and stored annotations are untouched by any of them.
</Info>

## Combinations that work

- **Dense scenes** (shelves, crowds): opacity down to ~50%, border 1x, labels off while drawing, on while reviewing.
- **Precision landmarking:** crosshair on, zoom high, anchors a step larger.
- **Screenshots and demos:** center labels on, border 2x, opacity up; geometry reads instantly.
