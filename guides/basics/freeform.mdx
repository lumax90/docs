Clicking vertices around a cloud, a pastry, a plant or a puddle is slow and joyless. The Freeform tool trades clicks for a single continuous stroke: press, trace the outline like a pencil, release, done. PixlHub converts the stroke into an editable shape.

<Info>
Available in image projects. Freeform shapes behave like polygons after creation: same class system, same vertex editing, same export geometry.
</Info>

## When freehand beats clicking

| Shape | Best tool |
|---|---|
| Rectangular-ish, speed first | [Bounding Box](/guides/basics/bounding-box) |
| Angular with clear corners (signs, buildings, packaging) | [Polygon](/guides/basics/polygon) |
| Organic and curvy (food, plants, fabric, people in soft poses) | **Freeform** |
| Linear structure, open path | [Line](/guides/basics/line) |

The demo below uses a supermarket meringue bag: crinkled plastic, no straight edge anywhere, exactly the shape vertex-clicking hates.

## Draw a freeform shape

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arm the class">
    Press the class hotkey (Product here) or click the class name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Freeform tool">
    Click the pen icon in the floating palette.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press, trace, release">
    Hold the mouse button down and follow the outline in one continuous motion. Releasing closes the shape automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Refine after the stroke

A freeform shape lands as an editable outline with vertex handles, so a wobbly section takes seconds to fix: switch to **Select**, click the shape, drag the offending handles.

<Frame caption="Selected freeform shapes expose handles like any polygon; imperfect strokes are corrected, not redrawn.">

</Frame>

## Stroke technique

- **Slow hand, smooth outline.** Stroke speed maps directly to vertex density; a slower trace follows detail better.
- **Zoom to the object first.** Freehand precision at 100 percent on a small object is luck, not skill.
- **Do not chase every wrinkle.** Guideline-level fidelity beats pixel heroics; a clean approximate outline trains better than a jittery exact one.
- **Fix, do not redo.** One rough section means dragging three handles, not redrawing the stroke.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a freeform shape different from a polygon in the data?">
    Structurally they are both closed outlines. Freeform is a faster input method for the same kind of geometry, so exports treat them alike. Details in <a href="/guides/essentials/exports">Exports</a>.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My strokes come out shaky. Any settings to help?">
    Zoom in further (canvas motion smooths relative to image pixels) and slow the stroke. A drawing tablet helps for all-day freehand work, though most annotators do fine with a mouse at higher zoom.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can AI propose the outline instead?">
    Yes. <a href="/guides/basics/ai-assisted-annotation">AI-assisted annotation</a> can generate an outline you only correct, which often beats hand-tracing on well-defined objects.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
